About Me!

Hello! My name is Faïca. I am 18 years old, and I own this blog. I became obsessed with reading after reading the “A Series of Unfortunate Events” books by Lemony Snicket in fourth grade. That was the first time I ever read for enjoyment instead of homework. I read the 13-book series in about two months, and I have been a reader ever since. I read in four languages (French, Haitian Creole, English, and Spanish).

When I put down the books

When I am not reading, I am usually studying; I am currently in my second year of college studying computer science. I also listen to a lot of music, and although I listen to all genres, I am a metalhead and a country fan at my core. I even own a CD collection; some of them I have inherited from my mom. She is who I get my love of music from. When I’m not doing any of those, I am probably watching a runway show on YouTube. I absolutely love fashion and watching runway shows, especially those from the 90s and 2000s. My favourite shows are Versace’s Fall/Winter Ready-to-Wear Collection from 2005 and Thierry Mugler’s Spring ‘97 couture collection.
